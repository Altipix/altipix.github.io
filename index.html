Stackly

A simple, satisfying block-stacking game for Android.

About the game

Tap to drop each block, line it up as perfectly as you can, and build the tallest tower you can. Earn coins, unlock themes, keep your daily streak going, and climb the leaderboards.

  • One-tap play, easy to learn and hard to put down
  • Collect coins and unlock new themes
  • Optional Google Play Games sign-in for leaderboards and cloud save
  • Free to play, supported by ads
