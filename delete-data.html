Delete your Stackly data

Stackly is published by Altipix. There is no Stackly account, so there is no account to close. Here is how to delete the data that exists.

Step 1: data on your phone

Your best score, coins, unlocked themes, streak and settings are stored on your phone. Uninstall Stackly, or clear its storage (Android Settings > Apps > Stackly > Storage > Clear data), and the copy on your phone is deleted. This does not delete the cloud save described in Step 2, if you use Play Games. We do not receive the data on your phone.

Step 2: Play Games scores and cloud save

If you signed in with Google Play Games, your leaderboard scores and a cloud save copy of your progress (records, coins and unlocked themes) are kept by Google, not by us. To delete them, open the Google Play Games app (or your Google Account settings), find Stackly under your games, and choose to delete its data or progress. Stackly's privacy policy explains this in the section "Your choices".

Step 3: statistics and advertising data

  • Stop sharing statistics: in Stackly open Settings and turn off Share stats.
  • Anonymous statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase) are linked only to an anonymous ID on your phone, never to your name or email. Google deletes them automatically after 14 months.
  • Advertising data is handled by Google AdMob. You can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's settings, and use the privacy choices in Stackly's Settings where they are shown.

Step 4: ask us to delete anything we hold

Email altipixsupport@gmail.com with the subject "Stackly data deletion request". We do not hold names, emails or accounts, and the anonymous statistics are linked only to a random ID on your phone, so we have no way to tell which data belongs to you. We will still reply, explain what exists and how you can remove it yourself, and delete anything we find that is linked to you. We answer as soon as we reasonably can, and within the time the law requires.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted by you: the data on your phone (Step 1). We never receive it, so there is nothing on our side to delete.
  • Nothing we can look up: we hold no name, email or account, so there is no personal record of you for us to find. If we ever do find data linked to you, we will delete it.
  • Kept by Google until you delete it: Play Games scores and cloud save (Step 2).
  • Kept for up to 14 months, then deleted automatically: anonymous analytics (Step 3).

Full details are in the Stackly Privacy Policy.
